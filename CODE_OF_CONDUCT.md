# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Paper Trail is a small open-source project, and everyone who
shows up here — to file an issue, review a change, or contribute code —
gets treated with respect regardless of who they are, where they're
from, or how much experience they have.

## What that means in practice

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements here are about the work, not
  about you — keep it that way.
- Feedback goes on the code, never on the person who wrote it.
- Harassment, personal attacks, and publishing someone's private
  information are never okay — not in issues, PRs, or anywhere else this
  project happens.
- If someone tells you a comment landed badly, believe them and adjust.
  Intent matters less than impact.

## When something goes wrong

If you see or experience behaviour that crosses the line, report it to
the maintainers through GitHub (a private security advisory or a direct
message works). Reports are handled in confidence; we don't share your
name with the person you're reporting.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or ban contributors whose
behaviour makes the space hostile. We'd rather lose a contribution than
lose contributors.
